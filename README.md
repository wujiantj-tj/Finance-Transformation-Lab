## ⭐ Featured AI projects

### AI Finance Weekly

**A bilingual, source-linked weekly intelligence briefing for Controllers and CFOs.**

The workflow researches current developments in AI, SAP, Finance technology and manufacturing digitalization, then turns selected stories into an English/German PDF briefing with business implications.

- **Research:** relevant technology and industry developments
- **Synthesis:** key takeaways for Controllers and CFOs
- **Traceability:** direct links to original sources
- **Delivery format:** bilingual PDF briefing

[Explore the AI for Finance project →](01_AI_for_Finance/README.md)

### LHT_HK_Formatter

**A specialist SAP Controlling formatter for TRUMPF LHT (Lohnherstellkosten) cost exports.**

- Detects CK13N, CK53N, ZCo6, ZCo9 or structural alternatives
- Defines a strict six-sheet Excel output contract
- Handles German number formats, BOM hierarchy and period comparisons
- Specifies supplier, operation, cost-summary and validation rules

[Explore the LHT_HK_Formatter project →](04_LHT_HK_Formatter/README.md) · [Open the private ChatGPT plugin](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugins_6ade2a48501c8191a3d355c267113d34)

[Explore the Business Presentation Advisor →](04_AI_Business_Presentation_Advisor/README.md) · [Open the private ChatGPT plugin](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugins_6ade270ff2448191843acc07b3f5ee6a)

### Expert Learning Mentor

**A Socratic learning assistant designed to build expert judgment, not memorization.**

- Explains five first-principles thinking models
- Compares three major debates and their assumptions
- Builds ten progressively harder, case-based questions
- Coaches one question at a time through guided discovery
- Reviews reasoning strengths, gaps and next learning steps
- Responds in the learner's language, including German

[Explore the Expert Learning Mentor →](04_Expert_Learning_Mentor/README.md) · [Open the private ChatGPT plugin](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugins_6ade2bcfa83c8191b7dbbd2a3af28b49)

### Film Expert Critic 🎬

**A film research and recommendation assistant** that accepts a partial title, year, actor, or director and returns a sourced film profile, IMDb rating/rank (clearly distinguished), one-sentence plot, similar-film recommendation, and child-suitability guidance. When a film is unsuitable for viewers under 14, the workflow uses a Peppa Pig placeholder instead of displaying that film's poster.

[Explore Film Expert Critic →](04_Film_Expert_Critic/README.md) · [Open the private ChatGPT plugin](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugins_6ade2c6470fc819194b4473a16612a83)
