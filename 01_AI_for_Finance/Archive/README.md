# AI Finance Archive

Historical AI Finance Weekly reports organized by publication date.

## Structure

Each folder is named by the report date (YYYY-MM-DD format):
- `2026-10-05/` - Reports from October 5, 2026
- `2026-09-28/` - Reports from September 28, 2026
- etc.

## Navigation

Use the date folders to find reports from specific weeks.
