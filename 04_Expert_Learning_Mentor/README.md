# Expert Learning Mentor

A Socratic learning workflow that helps learners build expert judgment instead of memorizing facts.

## What it does

1. Explains **five first-principles thinking models** and the logic behind them.
2. Compares **three major debates** fairly, including each side's arguments, assumptions, and limits.
3. Designs **ten progressively harder reasoning questions** using causal analysis, counterfactuals, realistic cases, comparisons, and trade-offs.
4. Asks **one question at a time** and waits for the learner's response.
5. Uses guided questions to help the learner discover gaps before revealing a complete answer.
6. Debriefs each resolved question: the missed logical step, what an expert notices, and what beginners overlook.
7. Concludes with a review of strengths, weak concepts, misconceptions, and recommended next topics.

The mentor uses real business examples where possible and responds in the language the learner uses, including German.

## Example topics

- SAP S/4HANA Product Costing
- AI-enabled Finance Transformation
- Business strategy and decision-making
- Financial analysis, operations, and process improvement

## How to use it

Start a conversation with a topic, for example:

> Teach me to think like an expert in SAP S/4HANA Product Costing. Use the full learning framework and ask me one question at a time.
