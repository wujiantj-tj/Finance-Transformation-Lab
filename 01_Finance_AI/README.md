Topics include:
- **AI Finance Weekly — Controller & CFO Briefing**
- Cost Analysis Agent

- Closing Assistant
- Finance Knowledge Agent

The focus is business value creation rather than technology itself.

## Featured project: AI Finance Weekly

**Purpose:** turn current developments in AI, SAP, enterprise software and manufacturing digitalization into a concise, decision-oriented briefing for Finance leaders.

**What it produces**
- Bilingual English/German weekly briefing for Controllers and CFOs
- Curated industry and technology developments with direct source links
- Controller/CFO implications and practical points to watch
- PDF output designed for sharing and review

**Workflow**
1. Research recent developments and check source dates.
2. Select the items relevant to Finance, SAP and manufacturing.
3. Summarize each item in English and German, with linked sources.
4. Add finance-oriented implications and package the briefing as a PDF.

**Example deliverable:** [AI Finance Weekly — 25 October 2026](Latest/AI_Finance_Weekly_2026-10-25.pdf)
